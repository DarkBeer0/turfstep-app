## TurfStep 1.4.1 — open beta

First public build. Single-player, no account needed, all data stays on your device.

### New in 1.4.1

- The position marker now keeps up with you and glides between GPS fixes; an arrow on its rim shows your direction
- Walking out and back is recorded properly
- The trail behind you fades out over 30 minutes
- Captured zones are drawn with a smooth outline; the message you left is shown first when you tap a zone
- Redesigned capture panel, centred achievement card, corrected app icon

### What's in it

- **Fog of war** — the map clears as you walk and stays cleared between sessions
- **Territory capture** — walk a closed loop to claim the area inside it
- **Profile** — distance walked, fog cleared, zones, ranks, achievements
- English, Russian and Polish interface; light and dark map

### Download

| File | For |
|---|---|

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Installation steps are in the [README](../../#install).

### Good to know

- Not on Google Play yet — Android will ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.
- An internet connection is needed for the base map (tiles from OpenFreeMap). Your location is not sent anywhere.
- GPS tracking with the screen off stops automatically after about 10 minutes without movement.
- Uninstalling the app removes your explored map and zones.
